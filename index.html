How To Play

🎯 Objective

Cyber Defender is an educational game where you play as a digital security analyst. Your job is to detect phishing emails, build strong passwords, identify safe websites, hunt down malware, and answer internet safety questions correctly.

🕹 Controls

  • Click or tap on the answer option you believe is correct.
  • Use the Pause button anytime to take a break.
  • Watch the timer — answering quickly earns combo bonuses.
  • You have a limited number of lives (hearts). Wrong answers cost a heart.

🏅 Scoring

  • Correct answers grant Score and XP points.
  • Consecutive correct answers build a Combo Multiplier.
  • Finishing a level with no mistakes unlocks bonus achievements.

📚 Levels

  • Level 1 — Phishing Email Detection
  • Level 2 — Password Strength Challenge
  • Level 3 — Safe Website Detector
  • Level 4 — Virus Hunter
  • Level 5 — Internet Safety Quiz
  • Level 6 — Final Cyber Mission (mixed challenges)
